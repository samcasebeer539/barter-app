# Win-Win Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

## What we collect

Account details: your first and last name, email address and password (your password is handled by our sign-in provider; we never see it). Profile details you choose to add: phone number, pronouns, bio and profile photo. What you post: item titles, descriptions and photos. Your saved safe exchange locations (a name, optional description, and map coordinates). Activity in the app: offers, questions and answers, trade moves, proposed meeting times and places, reviews and ratings, people you've blocked, and reports you make.

## Your device's location

Only if you turn on Location Services in Settings and allow it on your phone. It's used on your phone to center maps near you. It isn't sent to us or stored. Distances shown in the feed are worked out from saved exchange locations, not your live location.

## What other people can see

Other people using Win-Win can see your name, pronouns, bio, profile photo, rating and reviews, your posts, and your saved exchange locations (so you can agree where to meet). Your email and phone number are hidden unless you choose to show them on your profile. People you trade with see the questions, answers and moves in that trade. People you've blocked, and who've blocked you, can't see each other.

## How we use it

To run the app: show your posts and profile, carry out trades, work out distances, keep you signed in, prevent abuse, and review reports. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Who we share it with

Only the services that run the app for us: Google Firebase (sign-in and photo storage) and MongoDB Atlas (our database). They store it on our behalf. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

## How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account (Settings > Delete Account), we delete your sign-in, profile, posts and photos, and end your ongoing trades. Finished trades stay in the other person's trade history, and reviews you left stay, shown as from a "Former member". Reports may be kept to keep the community safe.

## Your choices

You can edit your profile and posts, hide your email and phone, turn Location Services off, block people, and delete your account at any time. To ask about or get a copy of your information, email us at the address in Settings > Help Center.

## Children

Win-Win is for people 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if you believe a child has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way, we'll let you know in the app.
